Understand Sonara Pack

How Sonara Pack works

Sonara Pack connects artists who publish music with creators who need tracks they can use in projects. The public catalogue lets people discover packs and tracks, hear previews and understand licensing terms before entering the Sonara experience.

Explore the catalogueDiscover Sonara Pack

Discover music

The catalogue organizes public releases by packs, tracks, artists and relevant categories. Public pages provide real musical context so a creator can more easily find a world suited to a video, film, game or another creative project.

Listen before choosing

When an audio preview is available, it can be played directly from the public page. Previews help evaluate a track before joining Sonara Pack to access the complete pack journey.

Understand the license

Each pack uses the Sonara Pack licensing system. The pack page shows the applicable terms: permitted uses, restrictions, any additional conditions and a credit requirement when the artist enables it.

Download and use

After joining Sonara Pack and accepting the applicable license, the creator can obtain the content according to the currently active mode. In Pre-V1, free acquisition continues to be handled by the existing system when this option is enabled.

For artists

Artists can build their catalogue with packs and tracks, define the licensing information provided by Sonara and benefit from public pages designed to make their releases discoverable on the web. The platform does not need a manual SEO intervention for every new publication.

Pre-V1 today

Sonara Pack is currently in Pre-V1. The catalogue, previews, licenses and downloads provided by this mode already exist, while real payments and commercial features remain disabled until commercial launch.
