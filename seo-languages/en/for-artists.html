For artists

Publish and license your music

Sonara Pack lets artists build a public catalogue of packs and tracks, with previews, licenses and discoverable pages. The platform is currently in Pre-V1: features available today are clearly separated from commercial features planned for V1.

Available now

Available now in Pre-V1

Artists can publish packs and tracks through the current flow, prepare their license, add catalogue information and obtain public pages for their releases. Published content automatically feeds the catalogue, internal links and existing SEO systems.

Build visibility

Each public release can become an entry point to the artist: pack page, eligible track pages, relevant categories, sitemap and public images. The goal is for the discovery surface to grow with the catalogue without requiring a manual SEO operation for every release.

Freedom to distribute

The current Sonara Pack flow does not turn publishing into platform exclusivity. The artist remains responsible for their rights and can continue exploiting their music elsewhere, provided this does not conflict with licenses they grant.

Planned for V1 / later

Planned for V1 / later

Real payments, commercial checkout and associated revenue remain disabled in Pre-V1. The currently planned commercial model is based on a Sonara Pack commission while the artist keeps the majority of sale revenue. Final terms will need to be presented clearly when commercial operation is activated.

Additional resources planned

The architecture also provides for V1 content types such as MIDI files and certain DAW projects. They should not be understood as commercial products that are already active today.

Build before commercial launch

Pre-V1 is used to prepare the catalogue, visibility, publishing habits and the platform before real payments open. An artist can therefore build a presence now without Sonara claiming that V1 sales are already available.
