For creators

Licensed music for your projects

Sonara Pack is designed for videographers, filmmakers, content creators, game developers, brands and creative teams that want to find music suited to their project with a clear usage framework.

Explore the catalogueJoin Sonara Pack

Search with context

Public pages do not rely only on a track title. When the data exists, Sonara also uses the artist, category, genre, mood and relevant uses to help discover music suited to the project.

Listen to previews

A preview lets you quickly assess the energy, atmosphere and musical direction of a track. The creator can then open the full pack to continue their choice in Sonara Pack.

Free listening is not a license

Being able to listen to music for free on a platform does not automatically give the right to use it in a video, film, advertisement, game or client project. Sonara Pack connects the music with a download and explicit licensing terms.

Download with a clear framework

Before using content, the creator sees the pack license and accepts its terms. Depending on the license, it can cover personal or commercial projects, monetization, social media, films, games, podcasts, live streaming and client work.

Respect the limits

The standard license notably prohibits resale of the sound on its own, redistribution, uploading it as a standalone music release, Content ID registration, sublicensing and false claims of ownership. The pack’s exact terms always remain the reference.

From catalogue to project

The journey stays simple: discover music, listen to its preview, check the license, join Sonara Pack, download the content and then use it within the permitted scope. The CTA to Sonara never bypasses language choice, the cinematic, registration or session resume.
